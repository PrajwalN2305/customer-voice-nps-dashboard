# Customer Voice & NPS Analytics - Business Insights Report

## Executive Summary
This document provides key business insights derived strictly from the cleaned analytical dataset (`5,914` survey responses across 2025). The overall Net Promoter Score (NPS) for the organization stands at **-22.6** (on a point scale of -100 to +100) with an Average CSAT of **3.07 / 5.0**, highlighting operational areas in support resolution SLAs and pricing structure that require targeted management intervention.

---

## 1. Key Business Insights

### Insight 1: Operational Resolution Latency Strongly Associated with Detractors
- **Finding:** Customers who logged Detractor scores (NPS 0–6) experienced significantly longer issue resolution times compared to Promoters.
- **Supporting Metric:** Detractors averaged **7.75 resolution days**, whereas Promoters averaged **1.40 resolution days**—a 5.5x increase in operational delay.
- **Business Interpretation:** Extended resolution cycles correlate directly with customer dissatisfaction. Accelerating tier-1 support response and reducing escalation bottlenecks is a critical operational priority to mitigate detractor volume.

---

### Insight 2: Pricing and Delivery Categories Drive Heavy Dissatisfaction
- **Finding:** Customer dissatisfaction is heavily concentrated in the **Pricing** and **Delivery** service categories, whereas **Product** and **Customer Support** maintain healthier sentiment baselines.
- **Supporting Metric:** Net NPS for Pricing stands at **-62.6** and Delivery at **-62.4**, compared to Customer Support (**-0.3**) and Product (**+3.2**).
- **Business Interpretation:** Feedback text indicates that customers perceive current pricing tiers as uncompetitive relative to feature rollout speed, while delivery delays create immediate post-purchase friction. Value proposition alignment and delivery logistics require strategic review.

---

### Insight 3: Mid-Market Segment Exhibits Lowest Satisfaction Score
- **Finding:** Mid-Market accounts report lower average satisfaction and Net NPS than Enterprise and SMB customer accounts.
- **Supporting Metric:** Mid-Market segment recorded Net NPS of **-23.5** (CSAT 3.07), whereas Enterprise recorded Net NPS of **-21.8** (CSAT 3.08) and SMB recorded Net NPS of **-21.9** (CSAT 3.06).
- **Business Interpretation:** Mid-Market accounts frequently experience friction between self-serve tools (geared for SMB) and dedicated account management (geared for Enterprise). Tailoring hybrid account support for Mid-Market accounts can bridge this experience gap.

---

### Insight 4: Regional Variance Highlights Underperformance in Asia-Pacific
- **Finding:** Customer satisfaction varies across geographical regions, with Asia-Pacific and Middle East & Africa exhibiting the lowest Net NPS scores.
- **Supporting Metric:** Asia-Pacific recorded Net NPS of **-24.3** (CSAT 3.01), and Middle East & Africa recorded **-23.6** (CSAT 3.03), compared to North America at **-22.2** (CSAT 3.10) and Latin America at **-17.8** (CSAT 3.14).
- **Business Interpretation:** Regional operational disparities—such as localized language support coverage, regional server latency, and delivery carrier SLAs—likely contribute to lower regional satisfaction scores.

---

### Insight 5: Phone Channel Processes Highest Proportion of Dissatisfied Customers
- **Finding:** Phone survey touchpoints record higher detractor ratios than digital channels (In-App, Email, Web).
- **Supporting Metric:** Phone channel responses recorded an Average CSAT of **3.02** and Detractor share of **49.4%**, whereas In-App survey responses recorded Average CSAT of **3.15** and Detractor share of **45.1%**.
- **Business Interpretation:** Customers opting for Phone support are frequently experiencing live workflow interruptions or urgent technical issues, resulting in lower immediate satisfaction scores at the moment of touchpoint interaction.
